1. Vocabulary Presentation

Notice and Sign Words

  • Notice = important information for everyone
  • Sign = written information that you can see
  • Warning = tells you about danger
  • Instructions = steps to follow
  • Rules = what you must and must not do
  • Emergency = dangerous situation that needs quick action

Action Words (Imperatives)

  • Enter = go inside
  • Exit = go outside/leave
  • Push = press with force
  • Pull = move toward you
  • Turn = move in a circle
  • Press = push gently
  • Wait = stay and do nothing
  • Stop = do not continue
  • Start = begin
  • Close = shut
  • Open = not closed

Office Equipment

  • Printer = machine that prints papers
  • Copier = machine that copies documents
  • Scanner = machine that copies documents to computer
  • Fax machine = sends documents by telephone
  • Coffee machine = makes coffee
  • Microwave = heats food
  • Elevator = moves people between floors
  • Fire extinguisher = puts out fires

Safety Words

  • Danger = not safe
  • Caution = be careful
  • Emergency = serious problem
  • Fire = burning
  • Smoke = gray clouds from fire
  • Exit = way out
  • First aid = help for injured people
  • Security = keeping safe

2. Reading: Workplace Notices

Notice 1: Printer Instructions
  1. Press the power button and wait for the green light.
  2. Place your document face-down on the glass.
  3. Select the number of copies you need.
  4. Press the start button and collect your copies.

Problems? Call ext. 205.

Notice 2: Coffee Machine
  1. Put your cup under the coffee spout.
  2. Choose your drink type (coffee, tea, etc.).
  3. Press the selected button and wait 30 seconds.

Please clean up after use. Thank you!

Notice 3: Fire Emergency Procedures
  • • Do not use elevators.
  • • Exit the building using the stairs.
  • • Meet at the assembly point in the parking lot.
  • • Do not return until security says it is safe.
  • • Call 911 from a safe location.
Notice 4: General Office Rules
  • • Please turn off all computers before leaving.
  • • Do not eat at your desk.
  • • Keep noise levels low during work hours.
  • • Park only in designated employee spaces.
  • • Report security problems to reception.

3. Grammar Presentation

Grammar for Instructions and Notices

1. Imperatives (Commands)

• Positive: Press the button. / Wait here.

• Negative: Do not enter. / Don't touch.

2. Sequencing Words

• First, press the button. Then, wait. Finally, take your cup.

3. Polite Instructions

• Please press the button.

• Please do not smoke.

4. Conditional Instructions

• If there is a fire, use the stairs.

• When the light is green, start.
